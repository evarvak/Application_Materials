\noindent Dear Mr. Bernard J. Kurtin, \newline \newline

\noindent I would like to thank you personally for establishing the Bernard Kurtin scholarship. 
I'm honored to be able to recieve the scholarship in the 2025-2026 academic year. It makes 
me very happy that my many hours of work and research are recognized by this award.
\newline

\noindent I am a graduate student at the University of Illinois Chicago. I study mathematics, 
and I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate. Like you, I believe strongly in the importance 
of math and science. To that end, I have sought to make an impact through mathematical research.
My work lies in Hodge theory, a field relevant to mathematicians and physicists alike,
which aims to relate linear algebra and geometry and addess some of the many unsolved
mathematical questions posed by string theory. \newline

\noindent Most of my work is performed with pen and paper, requiring little to no funding
beyond my income. However, a few practical aspects of my job demand a sizable budget.
I have already produced a paper, but to more effectively communicate my results to other 
researchers in the math community, I am required to frequently travel to conferences and 
workshops. Additionally, I will be applying for post-doc positions in the Fall 2026
semester, before which I will need to travel to different universities and present 
my work. \newline

\noindent To that end, the Bernard Kurtin scholarship will save me a lot of worry. I will be able 
to more easily afford waiting the time it takes for my travel expenses to be reimbursed; 
I will likewise have fewer issues finding funding for the few expenses that fall through
the reimbursement process, like food and conference fees. \newline

\noindent The scholarship has already had an impact on me. I have made plans to 
attend three different conferences within the next year, two of which are in Europe 
and would otherwise have required very careful planning. This award has made it 
noticably easier to make otherwise risky decisions in hopes of furthering my academic 
career. Once again, I thank you for establishing this scholarship, thereby enabling me
to focus on doing math.\newline \newline

\noindent Sincerely,\newline

\noindent Edward Varvak
