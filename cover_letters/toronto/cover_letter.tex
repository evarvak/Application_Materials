Dear Members of the Hiring Committee,

I am a graduate student at the University of Chicago, working in complex
algebraic geometry and Hodge theory under the direction of Benjamin Bakker,
and I expect to receive my Ph.D.\ in May 2027. I am applying for a Postdoctoral
Fellowship in Mathematics (\mbox{UofT-PDF}) beginning in Fall 2027. My
research concerns the monodromy and Hodge theory of Lagrangian fibrations on
hyper-K\"ahler and other symplectic varieties, as well as moduli spaces of
abelian varieties and their Mumford--Tate groups. At Toronto, I would be
particularly interested in working with Daniel Litt and Michael Groechenig.

Along with this letter, I include my curriculum vitae, publication list,
research statement, and teaching statement. Research letters will come from
Benjamin Bakker (Chicago), Radu Laza (Stony Brook), \.{I}zzet Co\c{s}kun
(UIC), and Valentino Tosatti (Courant), and a teaching letter from McKinley
Meyer (UIC). Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Edward A. Varvak
