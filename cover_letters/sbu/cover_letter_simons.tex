Dear Members of the Hiring Committee,

I am a graduate student at the University of Chicago, working in complex
algebraic geometry and Hodge theory under the direction of Benjamin Bakker,
and I expect to receive my Ph.D.\ in May 2027. I am applying for a James H.
Simons Instructorship (2027 Simons Lecturer) beginning in Fall 2027. My
research concerns the monodromy and Hodge theory of Lagrangian fibrations on
hyper-K\"ahler and other symplectic varieties, as well as moduli spaces of
abelian varieties and their Mumford--Tate groups. At Stony Brook, I would be
particularly interested in working with Radu Laza, Mark Andrea de Cataldo,
Christian Schnell, Ljudmila Kamenova, and Samuel Grushevsky.

Along with this letter, I include my AMS cover sheet, curriculum vitae,
publication list, research statement, and teaching statement. Research
letters will come from Benjamin Bakker (Chicago), Radu Laza (Stony Brook),
\.{I}zzet Co\c{s}kun (UIC), and Valentino Tosatti (Courant), and a teaching
letter from McKinley Meyer (UIC). Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Edward A. Varvak
