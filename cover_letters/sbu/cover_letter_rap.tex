Dear Members of the Hiring Committee,

I am a graduate student at the University of Chicago, working in complex
algebraic geometry and Hodge theory under the direction of Benjamin Bakker,
and I expect to receive my Ph.D.\ in May 2027. I am applying for a Research
Assistant Professorship at the Simons Center for Geometry and Physics
beginning in Fall 2027. My research concerns the monodromy and Hodge theory
of Lagrangian fibrations on hyper-K\"ahler and other symplectic varieties, as
well as moduli spaces of abelian varieties and their Mumford--Tate groups.

I hope to take part in the Simons Center's activities in geometry and
mathematical physics, including its workshops on Hodge theory,
hyper-K\"ahler geometry, and Higgs bundles. I would also be happy to help
organize workshops and programs there.
I would be particularly interested in working with Samuel Grushevsky 
at the Simons Center, and with Radu Laza, Mark Andrea de Cataldo, Christian Schnell, 
and Ljudmila Kamenova at Stony Brook University.

Along with this letter, I include my AMS cover sheet, curriculum vitae,
publication list, research statement, and teaching statement. Research
letters will come from Benjamin Bakker (Chicago), Radu Laza (Stony Brook),
\.{I}zzet Co\c{s}kun (UIC), and Valentino Tosatti (Courant), and a teaching
letter from McKinley Meyer (UIC). Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Edward A. Varvak
